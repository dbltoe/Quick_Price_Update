Quick Price Update -- changelog

v3.0.0 (2026-10-24)
  First release of this rewrite, numbered 3.0.0 to follow on from
  ianhg's Quick Price Update V2. A rewrite of the idea behind ianhg's Quick Price Update
  (Plugins Library 2338) and Quick Price & Quantity Update (2358), and
  Scott Wilson's Quick Quantity Update (1847), as an encapsulated plugin
  for Zen Cart 1.5.8a through 3.0.0 on PHP 7.4 through 8.5. No code from
  those plugins is reused and no core file is changed.

  - Catalog > Quick Price Update: find products by exact ID or ID ranges
    (12, 15, 40-55), by category with or without its subcategories, by
    name or model, and by status; sort and page the results.
  - Change prices and quantities in the table and save every changed row
    with one button. Values are checked before anything is written; a row
    someone else changed after the page loaded is skipped and reported.
  - Saving a price refreshes the storefront's price-sort value, so price
    sorting and "Starting at" prices stay right.
  - Specials, priced-by-attributes, call-for-price and always-free
    products are flagged, since shoppers don't pay the price shown as is.
  - A currency icon on Catalog > Categories/Products (product rows and
    category rows) opens that product, or that category with its
    subcategories. The icon follows the store's default currency.
